Reference desk notice

Legal information, not legal advice

Firmament is an educational and organizational portal. It does not provide legal representation, create confidentiality or privilege, predict outcomes, determine the truth of a claim, or establish that a form or procedure is correct for a specific court.

  • Confirm jurisdiction, deadlines, service rules, filing requirements, fees, and current law through official sources.
  • Consult a licensed attorney or qualified advocate about consequential decisions and individual circumstances.
  • Remove or redact Social Security numbers, account numbers, medical details, addresses, minor-child information, and other sensitive data before sharing files.
  • Do not upload confidential material into an unverified third-party service.
  • Several linked tools are local-first rather than fully offline and may load self-selected external fonts or public resources.
